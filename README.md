# Bike_shop_Dashboard
## 🚴 Toman Analytics: Bike Shop Sales & Rider Insights Dashboard

The Toman Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report built to explore bike shop sales data across different time periods. It highlights key metrics such as revenue, profit, rider trends, and seasonal performance, helping stakeholders identify profitable time periods and optimize business strategies.

### Tech Stack
The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

📊 Power BI Desktop – For creating interactive dashboards and visualizations

🔄 Power Query – For data cleaning, transformation, and preprocessing

🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – For calculated measures and KPIs (profit, revenue, riders)

🗂️ Data Modeling – To establish relationships and enable cross-filtering

🛢️ SQL – For data extraction, querying, and initial data manipulation

📁 File Format - .pbix for development, .sql for data querying and analysis and .png for dashboard preview

### Data Source

The dataset used for this dashboard contains bike shop sales and rider data, including over 10,000+ records of hourly and monthly transactions. It captures data across multiple years (2021–2022), including 3M total riders, revenue of approximately $15M, and profit of around $10M. The dataset includes key attributes such as date, hour, revenue, profit, rider type (registered vs casual), and seasonal classification. This structured data enables detailed time-based and demographic analysis of business performance.

### Features / Highlights
• Business Problem

Bike rental businesses often struggle to identify peak revenue hours, seasonal trends, and rider behavior patterns. Without proper insights, it becomes difficult to optimize pricing, staffing, and marketing strategies.

• Goal of the Dashboard

To create an interactive dashboard that provides insights into revenue trends, rider demographics, and seasonal performance, enabling better decision-making and business optimization.

• Walkthrough of Key Visuals

KPI Cards (Top Section)
Displays total riders (3M), profit ($10M), revenue ($15M), and profit margin (0.45)

Hourly Sales Table
Shows revenue distribution across hours, identifying peak earning times (midday & evening)

KPI Over Time (Line Chart)
Tracks riders, profit, and revenue trends across months for 2021–2022

Revenue by Season (Bar Chart)
Highlights seasonal performance, showing which seasons generate the most revenue

Rider Demographics (Donut Chart)
Compares registered vs casual riders, showing majority contribution from registered users

• Business Impact & Insights

📈 Peak revenue occurs during midday and evening hours (10 AM – 6 PM)

📊 Registered riders (~81%) contribute significantly more than casual riders

💰 Revenue shows steady growth from 2021 to 2022

🌦️ Certain seasons (Season 3) generate the highest revenue (~$4.9M)

📅 Mid-week days like Wednesday and Friday show higher sales

📉 Early morning and late-night hours have lower revenue contribution
